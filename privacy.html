1. Introduction

Keyword Diary is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how your personal information is collected, used, and disclosed by Keyword Diary.

Your data is encrypted using industry-standard AES-256 encryption. The encryption key is stored securely in your device's secure storage (Keychain on iOS, Keystore on Android).
